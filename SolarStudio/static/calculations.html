Geometry and orientation

The model uses metres and +Z up. The compass input is the bearing of true north in model coordinates. The directed-edge rotation below converts model coordinates to east, north and up.

bm=atan2⁡(Δx,Δy),Δ=btrue−bmb_m=\operatorname{atan2}(\Delta x,\Delta y),\quad \Delta=b_{\rm true}-b_m
E=xcos⁡Δ+ysin⁡Δ,N=−xsin⁡Δ+ycos⁡Δ,U=zE=x\cos\Delta+y\sin\Delta,\quad N=-x\sin\Delta+y\cos\Delta,\quad U=z
β=arccos⁡(clamp⁡(nU,−1,1)),As=atan2⁡(nE,nN) mod 360∘\beta=\arccos(\operatorname{clamp}(n_U,-1,1)),\quad A_s=\operatorname{atan2}(n_E,n_N)\bmod360^\circ

A horizontal surface has no surface azimuth. The villa’s south and west design pitches have 25° tilt and true azimuths of 180° and 270° with the supplied alignment.

Weather and solar position

The annual run uses NASA POWER hourly GHI, DNI and DHI in UTC. Hour stamps mark the start of the interval. The computation uses pvlib SPA at each midpoint, including leap-day hours.

tmid=tstart+Δt2,G=HintervalΔt,Φ=90∘−αt_{\rm mid}=t_{\rm start}+\frac{\Delta t}{2},\quad G=\frac{H_{\rm interval}}{\Delta t},\quad \Phi=90^\circ-\alpha

The following course approximations are useful for independent checks. Angles are in degrees, longitudes are east-positive and ET is in minutes.

δ=23.45∘sin⁡[360∘365(284+Nd)],B=360∘364(Nd−81)\delta=23.45^\circ\sin\left[\frac{360^\circ}{365}(284+N_d)\right],\quad B=\frac{360^\circ}{364}(N_d-81)
ET=9.87sin⁡(2B)−7.53cos⁡B−1.5sin⁡BET=9.87\sin(2B)-7.53\cos B-1.5\sin B
AST=tstandard+ET+4(λ−λstd)60,h=15∘(AST−12)AST=t_{\rm standard}+\frac{ET+4(\lambda-\lambda_{\rm std})}{60},\quad h=15^\circ(AST-12)
α=arcsin⁡(sin⁡Lsin⁡δ+cos⁡Lcos⁡δcos⁡h)\alpha=\arcsin(\sin L\sin\delta+\cos L\cos\delta\cos h)
A=[atan2⁡(sin⁡h,sin⁡Lcos⁡h−cos⁡Ltan⁡δ)+180∘] mod 360∘A=\bigl[\operatorname{atan2}(\sin h,\sin L\cos h-\cos L\tan\delta)+180^\circ\bigr]\bmod360^\circ

Incidence, shading and irradiance

The sun uses apparent elevation. An outward-offset ray tests each front-facing patch. Every imported triangle can cast shade, including hidden display layers.

s=(cos⁡αsin⁡A,cos⁡αcos⁡A,sin⁡α),c=n⋅s\mathbf s=(\cos\alpha\sin A,\cos\alpha\cos A,\sin\alpha),\quad c=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s
GPOA=DNImax⁡(0,c)Vsun+DHIFsky+0.20 GHIFgroundG_{\rm POA}=\mathrm{DNI}\max(0,c)V_{\rm sun}+\mathrm{DHI}F_{\rm sky}+0.20\,\mathrm{GHI}F_{\rm ground}
Fsky,open=1+cos⁡β2,Fground,open=1−cos⁡β2F_{{\rm sky,open}}=\frac{1+\cos\beta}{2},\quad F_{{\rm ground,open}}=\frac{1-\cos\beta}{2}
FD=FD,open∑jVjmax⁡(0,n⋅ωj)∑jmax⁡(0,n⋅ωj)F_{\mathcal D}=F_{\mathcal D,\rm open}\frac{\sum_j V_j\max(0,\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\omega_j)}{\sum_j\max(0,\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\omega_j)}

The app samples 256 directions per hemisphere. The model assumes isotropic sky diffuse, uniform Lambertian ground reflection and ground albedo 0.20. Live playback uses an illustrative clear sky. The annual design map uses NASA POWER weather.

Annual integration and exports

The app integrates hourly contributions once, uses area weights for face averages, and reports direct beam loss separately from total loss.

Hi=11000∑tGPOA,i,tΔtt,Hˉface=∑iaiHi∑iaiH_i=\frac{1}{1000}\sum_t G_{{\rm POA},i,t}\Delta t_t,\quad \bar H_{\rm face}=\frac{\sum_i a_iH_i}{\sum_i a_i}
lossi=100(1−HiHi,open)%,tdirect=∑tΔttVsun,t1ct>0\mathrm{loss}_i=100\left(1-\frac{H_i}{H_{i,\rm open}}\right)\%,\quad t_{\rm direct}=\sum_t\Delta t_t V_{{\rm sun},t}\mathbf1_{c_t>0}
ui=clamp⁡(Hi−Hmin⁡Hmax⁡−Hmin⁡,0,1)u_i=\operatorname{clamp}\left(\frac{H_i-H_{\min}}{H_{\max}-H_{\min}},0,1\right)

The annual patch CSV reports geometry, weather coverage, exposure and losses across the building. The selected patch hourly CSV traces each hour from weather and sun position to its plane-of-array components. A flat surface has a blank CSV azimuth. The panel planner estimates annual energy from the annual irradiation of each module footprint and an aggregate performance ratio.
